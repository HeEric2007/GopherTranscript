Save your lecture videos as text

Get a readable transcript of lecture videos on UNITE and Canvas, or every UNITE lecture in a course in one download. Good for screen readers, text-to-speech, translation, and searching your notes.

Get started

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:
    GopherTranscript drag me up there

    Don't see a bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac.

    Can't drag it? Add it by hand
    2. Right-click your bookmarks bar and choose Add page… (Chrome, Edge) or Add Bookmark… (Firefox).
    3. Name it GopherTranscript, paste the code into the URL field, and save.
  2. Log in and open a lecture on UNITE or Canvas.
  3. Click GopherTranscript in your bookmarks bar. On UNITE you can save a whole course at once; on Canvas it's one video at a time. See UNITE steps and Canvas steps below.

On UNITE

The panel changes depending on which UNITE page you're on. You can close it with × and bring it back by clicking the bookmark again.

On a course page

Saves every lecture into one .zip, oldest first, numbered so they stay in order. A full semester takes a few minutes. Stop saves whatever is done so far.

On Canvas

For lecture videos posted on Canvas pages, like a week's lecture links. Canvas keeps each video in a protected frame, so saving one takes two clicks, and there's no Save all on Canvas.

First, on the Canvas page

Click GopherTranscript. The panel lists the videos on that page. Press Open video next to the one you want, and it opens by itself in a new tab.

Privacy

  • Everything runs in your own browser, using the UNITE or Canvas login you already have. It never sees your password.
  • It only downloads the caption files your video player already uses. No tracking, no accounts.

Good to know

  • Transcripts come from auto-generated captions, so expect mistakes with names, math, and code.
  • They're for your own studying. Please don't post or share them; lectures belong to your instructors.
  • Saving a transcript doesn't count as watching. It won't mark lecture attendance or complete in-video quizzes.
  • If it can't find captions, press play for a few seconds and click the bookmark again.
